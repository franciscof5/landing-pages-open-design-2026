## Color roles

- **Background** (`#ffffff`) — background: page canvas
- **Surface** (`#f5f5f5`) — surface: cards / raised surfaces
- **Foreground** (`#000000`) — foreground: body text
- **Muted** (`#8c8c8c`) — muted: secondary text
- **Border** (`#dbdbdb`) — border: hairlines / dividers
- **Accent** (`#ffd700`) — accent: primary brand color
- **Accent secondary** (`#c8102e`) — accent-secondary: secondary brand color / links
